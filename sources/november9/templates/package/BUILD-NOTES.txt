Forceware 382.69 (11-9-2026)

- Fixed black-and-white bars and display corruption on DDR4 GT 1030 cards.
- Improved GP107/GP108 power management, fan-controller handling and cleanup after failed initialization.
- Enabled GP107 DisplayPort HBR3 selection and improved detection of monitor timings.

When upgrading, select **Custom (Advanced) → Perform a clean installation**, then restart.
