HDMI clock handling

Eligible Fermi, Kepler and Maxwell 1 HDMI paths allow up to 340 MHz,
subject to source, connector, adapter and monitor limits. Maxwell 2 and
Pascal retain their HDMI 2.0 handling, with a 594 MHz policy ceiling.
DVI remains subject to its separate single-link/dual-link restrictions.

GT 640 output was confirmed at 3440x1440/60, 319.75 MHz. A 340 MHz
custom timing gave no picture on that test configuration; the cause was
not established. Pascal HDMI output was confirmed at 543.5 MHz.
Exact-ceiling operation is not guaranteed on every board or monitor.
HDMI has no automatic DisplayPort-style link-quality fallback.
